FICTIONAL DEMO SOURCE. Ivy interviews Nova after a 2-1-loss-2 weekend at the
Sample GP. Nova is upbeat but candid about the Race 3 lock-up that handed the win
to Vega, calling it "a reset, not a pattern" and pointing to strong long-run pace
as the real story of the weekend. On the title fight, Nova says leading from the
front suits them and expects the advantage to hold on higher-speed circuits, while
conceding Vega is the tougher wheel-to-wheel racer on street tracks. Looks ahead to
the next round hoping for dry conditions and a clean qualifying.
