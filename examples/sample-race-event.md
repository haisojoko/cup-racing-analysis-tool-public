**Sample GP**

FICTIONAL DEMO SOURCE. Round 1 of the demo season. 10 drivers attended. Nova and
Vega were the clear front two. Nova took pole and converted R1 and R2 from the
front. In R3, Nova locked up at the first corner chasing clean air; Vega took the
win and Comet recovered to third. R4 went back to Nova after a clean start.
Nova finished 1-1-2-1, Vega 2-2-1-2, Comet a steady 3rd on the day. Close season
expected.
